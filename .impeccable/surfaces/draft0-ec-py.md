# Emotion Rush game screens

Scope: every screen of the fullscreen OpenCV game (`DRAFT0_EC.py`) except the transcript, which follows the SIX ITB reference. Visitor mode: Experience.

Audience and task: ITB Insight 2026 booth visitors, 1–3 faces, watched by friends standing 1–3 m behind them on a landscape TV. They play seven expression courses, pick six photos and save a strip.

Constraints: OpenCV window with Pillow-rendered TTF type; mouse and keyboard; strip template fixed; camera image is never tinted or posterised (it becomes the printed strip).

User steer (2026-11-17): chose Mading Himpunan; the board must not feel empty; keep it balanced across the screen, at Persona 5's level of finish. Craft bar and borrowing limits: `.impeccable/reference/persona5-transfer.md`.

Memorable moment: the grade stamp slammed onto the player's photo.

Unresolved: grade scale (A+/B+ vs ITB A/AB/B/BC), on-screen language mix, course codes.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The screen is a himpunan notice board that never stops being pinned: menus, courses and grades arrive as paper slammed onto the board. It refuses the photobox default of a centred camera, pill buttons and confetti.

OWN-WORLD: ITB Insight royal-blue felt board #1B23C9 edge to edge, with faded torn notices and staple holes in deeper blue; white paper strips and photo prints with soft paper shadows; black condensed Archivo; stabilo yellow #FFE14A, pink #FF3D8B, orange #FF8A00 and green #34C759 only on the active item; pink pins, beige tape, pink stamp ink.
